## What it does

A journaling/article platform with real accounts behind it: users log in,
write entries, attach photos, and manage their own content through a
dashboard, with a separate admin view for moderation.

## Structure

- **Authentication** — login and session handling (`login.php`,
  `logout.php`) rather than a single shared page.
- **Content management** — entries are stored and served dynamically
  (`article.php`, `article_data.php`) instead of hard-coded pages.
- **Admin dashboard** — a separate view (`admin.php`, `dashboard.php`)
  for oversight of published content.
- **Media handling** — photo uploads (`upload_foto.php`) attached to
  entries.

## Why it matters here

This was the project where server-side logic, sessions, and a real
database stopped being theoretical — the first time "the backend" meant
code I'd written and had to keep straight, not just a concept from a
course. It's a deliberately unglamorous CRUD app, and that's the point:
the fundamentals of auth, persistence, and access control live here.
